Public web access does not establish redistribution rights. A SHA-256 match establishes byte identity with a submitted file, not publisher authenticity, timestamp accuracy or legal permission. The operator and independent reviewer must verify those facts against original publisher and contractual evidence.

No provider keys, feed licences, or source release files are stored in this repository. The application therefore reports `DATA_UNAVAILABLE` for all Indian inputs in isolated staging.
