# Assignment 5: actor-response research methodology

## Source and access boundary

The [Reserve Bank Innovation Hub DBIE mirror](https://dbie.rbihub.in/) is a **secondary, experimental** copy of RBI data. Its [read-only data API documentation](https://dev.dbie.rbihub.in/docs/using-the-site) explicitly describes unauthenticated JSON/CSV access. This implementation makes bounded GET requests to three named tables: [scheduled commercial bank aggregates](https://data-api.dbie.rbihub.in/api/tables/financial_sector/r901_scheduled_commercial_banks_select_aggregates), [RBI liquidity operations](https://data-api.dbie.rbihub.in/api/tables/financial_markets/r702_liquidity_operations_by_rbi), and [weekly foreign-exchange reserves](https://data-api.dbie.rbihub.in/api/tables/external_sector/r1_foreign_exchange_reserves_weekly). The [project repository](https://github.com/Reserve-Bank-Innovation-Hub/dbie.rbihub.in) says its *code* is MIT licensed; that is **not** a grant of rights to republish RBI observations. All imported rows remain private research evidence. No governed/public Observation row is created.

The mirror returns a load time, DBIE path and row-level references. These are not the original RBI publication timestamp or publisher vintage. `published_at` is null; `retrieved_at` records the actual system retrieval. `source_known` therefore cannot show a row before retrieval unless a separately verified publication timestamp is later added. Retrospective analysis with an episode end and a current knowledge cutoff is labelled `retrospective_current_vintage`. Source data may contain later revisions. The mirror itself warns that coverage can lag RBI; verify important conclusions against original releases before public display or portfolio use.

The adapter validates exact table names, reported frequency, column headers, units, row order/count and numeric formats. It supports a configurable 2018-onward private backfill and idempotent changed-value vintages. Bank credit and aggregate deposits are ₹ crore outstanding stocks, not flows or loan demand. The imported liquidity column (`r702_liquidity_operations_by_rbi`, column c18) is RBI's published daily **net** figure across mixed operation types under the publisher label "Net Injection (+)/ Absorption (-) (1+3+5+7+10-2-4-6-9)": positive means net injection and negative means net absorption by publisher convention only — the mirror cannot independently verify RBI's sign convention or formula, and instrument-level gross operations and maturities are not imported, so offsetting operation types can cancel inside the published net. The summed value is a cumulative signed net of reported daily operation flows, not a change in outstanding liquidity and not a liquidity stock; summing several daily observations is bookkeeping over a fixed reporting window, not an economically meaningful liquidity quantity. Reporting-convention or definition changes could create false signals, and the definition was not independently validated, so the liquidity interpretation is classified `unresolved_definition` in the evaluated result. Aggregate operations must not be read as an intentional RBI response to a bank funding gap without independently dated policy rationale. Weekly gross reserves are US$ millions; their changes do not prove intervention. The bank table notes a July 2023 bank merger and a December 2025 change to RBI's fortnight definition. Year-on-year transforms crossing these dates are withheld, and the actor detector marks such onsets `DEFINITION_REVIEW`.

In the 1 November 2026 retrieval, the bank table's mirror catalogue `period_to` field lagged some visible bank rows. The adapter flags those rows in its reliability note for original-release cross-check; it does not treat catalogue metadata as original publication evidence.

## Economic interpretation

`config/actor_research.v1.yaml` is a versioned economic hypothesis. It names the disturbance (bank-credit growth above deposit growth), actors and incentives, observed RBI operation proxy, proposed reinforcing and balancing mechanisms, transmission channels, alternatives, confirmation and falsification conditions. Source records stay in `ResearchEvidence`; the interpretation and evidence identifiers live in the evaluated result. A frozen `DiscoverySnapshot` stores the exact actor, discovery and transformation rules plus input vintages. A human can open a provisional `DiscoveryHypothesis`; repeated evaluations of the same onset retain one active investigation. Nothing is promoted into the six-cycle catalog.

The detector calculates fortnightly credit and deposit YoY growth against the closest prior-year reporting date within 17 days. It requires two nonpositive funding-gap reports followed by two consecutive positive-gap reports no more than 35 days apart. It then checks the first five **later** dated RBI liquidity-operation observations within 45 days. The signed sum is only a description of those five daily operations, not a stock of liquidity. Two later bank reports within 90 days test whether the gap widens, narrows or closes. Daily and fortnightly observations are aligned by explicit chronology; they are not treated as interchangeable frequencies. The thresholds screen research leads and have not been calibrated as probabilities or investment weights.

Outputs are `POTENTIAL_REINFORCEMENT`, `WEAKENING_REINFORCEMENT`, `EMERGING_INFLECTION`, `CONTRADICTORY_EVIDENCE`, `CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, `DEFINITION_REVIEW`, `STALE_EVIDENCE`, `HISTORICAL_ONLY` or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Even the first three are **provisional interpretations**. Credit and deposits share a source table, and an RBI operation's motive is not observed. A genuine actor-response mechanism needs independently dated policy rationale, borrower or bank behavior, and repeated follow-up evidence. The UI separates observed values from that proposed interpretation, alternatives, missing confirmation, 1–3 and 3–6 month conditions, and the original source references.

The existing cross-indicator discovery questions still work. This actor framework is a separate configurable mechanism; a new economic question can be added without creating a seventh established cycle. No public cycle score, brokerage link or directional probability is changed.

## Reproduction

For isolated non-demo development or staging only, run migrations on an independent database, then `python -m packages.jobs.rbi_mirror_research --table all --start-date 2018-01-01`. The existing private research API and `/intelligence/discovery` can then inspect the results. `python scripts/actor_response_demo.py` instead fetches actual mirror rows into **in-memory SQLite**, evaluates selected episodes, prints provenance and discards the database. Do not use mirror data in production or publicly redistribute it until rights, source definitions and original release timing have been independently reviewed.

Capital-flow and savings-actor responses remain important gaps. The mirror search did not reveal current FPI/DII or SIP flow series. NSDL, NSE and AMFI access and reuse terms need separate resolution; site visibility alone does not authorize automated archival or public display. Similarly, the mirror's bank/liquidity timing cannot establish whether RBI acted *because* of bank credit conditions.
