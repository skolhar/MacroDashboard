# Assignment 2 cycle intelligence — corrected PR #16 review

## Existing architecture and causal boundary

The public v2 stress monitor still uses fixed cycle weights and multivariate threshold alerts. Those scores describe stress, not causal feedback or market forecasts. The six hypotheses in config/cycle_mechanisms.v1.yaml remain separate from that scoring path. This work follows the discovery-first guidance in Issue #9 and open PR #15 without editing PR #15.

The research_evidence table now stores raw private observations only: stable indicator key, measurement definition, typed numeric or qualitative value, period, cadence, source, retrieval time, optional publication time, source revision, limitations and permitted private-use basis. It contains no cycle, mechanism or stance. The research_interpretations table stores append-only analyst assessments linked by observation ID, including cycle and mechanism, evidence type, stance, causal rationale, alternative explanations, revision and operator identity. A feedback-link interpretation must link a prior, independently identified driver observation to an actor-response observation. A third independent trend observation is needed to assess reinforcement as supported. This remains a tentative hypothesis, not proven causality.

For compatibility, the original PR #16 evidence POST still accepts its optional cycle/mechanism/stance fields, but explicitly reports that they were ignored. It assigns a unique legacy indicator key if none was supplied; an analyst must submit a separate reasoned interpretation. The engine selects source vintages by stable indicator key, source and period, not a free-text description. Conflicting values for the same series and period, or opposing interpretations of one observation, are displayed as conflicts and excluded from decisive inference. Missing, stale, directional-only and contradictory evidence have distinct states.

Private research does not require the governed release archive, two-person approval or proof of exact historical publication clocks. Operators still document permitted use and limitations. A free-text use note is not a legal rights determination. Existing governed release and demo pipelines are unchanged. The published PR revision research_1 is pinned to its original schema. Additive revision research_2 follows it, separates raw facts from interpretations and adds immutable assessment snapshots. Existing isolated research_1 rows are retained as raw facts with unique legacy indicator keys; their old stances become clearly labelled legacy trend interpretations without a causal link, recorded at migration time rather than backdated. Neither migration has run on production. The research_2 downgrade refuses to erase provenance; restore a reviewed backup if rollback is required.

## Versioned rules and chronology

config/cycle_evaluation.v2.yaml defines cadence windows: daily 7, weekly 21, monthly 75, quarterly 210, annual 450 and event 90 calendar days. These are provisional freshness policies, not economic-effect weights. Each mechanism reports supported, contradicted, conflicting, directional-only, stale or missing with exact observation and interpretation identifiers.

A reinforcing loop needs a reasoned feedback link involving two independently identified series, plus a separate trend series. Two supportive trends do not establish a loop. Two distinct fresh feedback-link periods can mark an initial mature/persistent state; one can mark emerging. A new supported link after a dated emerging assessment can mark strengthening.

Weakening and reversing require a previously persisted assessment under the same catalog and rule versions that established reinforcement before new evidence was retrieved. A later interpretation must identify a broken feedback link; corrective or policy-response evidence must follow that break. One broken link and a sequenced corrective response indicate weakening. Reversing additionally needs two distinct broken-link periods and two distinct corrective periods after the break. A candidate needs corrective responses from at least two later periods. An ordinary trend reversal, pre-existing balancing force, absent observation or changed definition cannot satisfy this sequence. Conflicting evidence cannot create an outlook.

The evaluator filters observations by retrieval and known publication time, interpretations by assessment time, and market bars by retrieval time. Unknown original publication time permits present-day exploratory use after SmartHedge retrieval but never historical backdating. An ordinary as_of query is labelled a recomputation with current rules. Historical rule-version replay uses cycle_assessment_snapshots: every snapshot preserves its dated result, exact catalog and rule content, selected observation and interpretation IDs, and their revisions. The assessment-by-ID API returns the stored result unchanged after later source or catalog revisions. Snapshot creation is explicit; missing historical snapshots are not manufactured.

Yahoo bars enter through the provider-neutral history and status APIs. Price changes can describe market response but never create an economic thesis. The outlook marks missing, incomplete, stale and failed market confirmation. WTI and Brent are global futures proxies, distinct from MCX crude; Yahoo FX differs from RBI/FBIL published reference rates. Instrument and horizon influence is qualitative and uncalibrated.

## Private access and audit

Research API reads require research:read; evidence, interpretation, snapshot and investigation writes require research:write. Existing DB credentials support expiry and revocation. New facts, interpretations, assessments and investigation state changes create operator audit entries. Revisions append instead of overwriting. Production and demo research endpoints remain disabled.

The Next.js /intelligence route rejects requests if its separate UI user/password or server-side read token is missing. When configured, middleware checks HTTP Basic authentication for page and action requests before rendering. Staging Compose passes STAGING_RESEARCH_UI_USER, STAGING_RESEARCH_UI_PASSWORD and a read-only STAGING_RESEARCH_OPERATOR_TOKEN to the web container. Caddy remains loopback-bound. Starting an investigation also requires a separately entered write-scoped token. Keep credentials unique to isolated staging and rotate them under the existing operator procedure. Do not expose Basic authentication without TLS on an untrusted network.

Workflow: POST /api/v1/research/evidence records a raw fact; POST /api/v1/research/interpretations records a reasoned stance and alternatives; POST /api/v1/research/assessments records an immutable assessment; GET /api/v1/research/overview recomputes with current rules; GET /api/v1/research/assessments/{id} replays a stored assessment. Operators can open and update investigations without promoting a new cycle automatically.

## Actual-source demonstration and limits

A read-only official U.S. Treasury XML request on 1 November 2026 returned 21 October 10-year par-yield observations. The last dated 30 October was 4.94%. The research_treasury_example script loaded it as a raw numeric fact in memory, with publication time unknown, and correctly returned indeterminate for the global AI/credit cycle. No causal stance was assigned, no permanent database was changed, and no public redistribution right was inferred. All regression economic values are synthetic fixtures.

Analysts still supply causal rationale and alternatives; neither a schema nor paired readings prove causation. Feedback-link and freshness rules are provisional. The first assessment must be recorded prospectively before transition detection; historical backfills alone cannot manufacture a prior regime. Market expectations, ETF NAV effects and longer-horizon outcomes remain unresolved. The next assignment can add reviewed anomaly baselines and prospective outcome tracking without rewriting stored snapshots.
