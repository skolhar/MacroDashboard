# Phase 3B historical coverage and gap report

Target window: 1 January 2018 through the reporting timestamp. The read-only `python -m scripts.phase3b_gap_report` command runs only in development, demo or staging. It reports each selected indicator's system-known and source-known counts, explicit absent months, exact absent dates only for verified daily trading calendars, unverified calendar years, all retained vintages and unreviewed periods. Save its JSON output with the review inventory; rerun after each authorised release import.

**Current isolated staging inventory:** no RBI, FBIL, NSDL, AMFI or licensed Brent releases have been approved or imported. Thus all Indian and Brent indicators in this report have zero verified observations and all months since January 2018 are gaps in both reconstruction modes. The only previously connected source-backed observation is U.S. Treasury 10Y, outside this Indian gap report. There is no basis for 2018/2020/2022/2024–2026 FX backtest, forward-adjusted reserves, or claims of alert lead time. The monitor must show Indian data as unavailable.

The 2018–2025 India market calendars have not been independently reviewed in this repository; 2027 is explicitly unverified. Exact missing business days and multi-year continuity therefore remain unknown for those years. Calendar maintenance is a prerequisite for five-year session-based transformations and calibration, even after lawful historical releases are obtained. A source-released row may appear in `source_known` before its later ingestion, but remains absent from `system_known` until actual ingestion. This is an intentional distinction, not missing data silently filled in.

To move an indicator out of this gap state, obtain the publisher's original historical releases, independent evidence of each publication timestamp and vintage, the specific storage/processing/display entitlement, and an approved definition mapping. A complete source archive may require multiple release vintages for the same economic period. The review workflow must record gaps and conflicting sources explicitly; do not interpolate missing values or backdate ingestion.
